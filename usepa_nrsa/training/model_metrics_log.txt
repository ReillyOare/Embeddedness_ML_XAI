**This Txt File was created to keep track of the results from various model runs for the EPA 2018-2019 dataset to provide evidence for the efficacy of the final model selected.**

**Scenario 1:** 
Incoming dataset:
a) Feature Transformed with power transformers, etc.
b) Trimmed to only include embeddedness <95% (~900 data points)
c) Embeddedness was transformed using a quantile normal power transformer (then later inversed)
d) LAT/LON were included and not transformed
e) DBSCAN clusters were used as category dtypes with hyperparameters (eps=0.0187, min_samples=3)
f) Distance to Centroid was included as float64 and not transformed
g) Level 1 Ecoregions included as one-hot encoded features
